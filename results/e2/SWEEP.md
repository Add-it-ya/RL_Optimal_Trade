- Experiments: e2_ic000, e2_ic025, e2_ic050, e2_ic100, e2_ic200; metric `objective_bps`.
- `improvement` is how much cheaper the strategy is than the benchmark, paired episode by episode (positive = better). For multi-seed agents the 95% CI is across training seeds; `p_holm` is Holm-adjusted over every row of both tables.
- `ic` is the target information coefficient of the order-book signal; real signals sit around 0.05-0.10 (shaded in the figure).
